 My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

![Stranger Things Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/f/f7/Stranger_Things_season_4.jpg)

Stranger Things is one of my favorite Netflix series.  
It’s a mix of *science fiction, horror, and nostalgia from the 1980s.

 Main Characters
- Eleven – A girl with telekinetic powers.
- Mike Wheeler – Eleven’s close friend and leader of the group.
- Jim Hopper – The chief of police in Hawkins.
- Dustin, Lucas, and Will – Loyal friends who help uncover the town’s mysteries.

Why I Like It
1. The storyline is unpredictable and full of suspense.  
2. The soundtrack gives strong 80s vibes.  
3. Each season expands the mysterious world of the *Upside Down*.  

> “Friends don’t lie.” — Eleven
